### 1. "Kill off Harlan Oduya within the next few episodes."

Given after episode 4, for the rest of the story.

**What the planner changed:** Harlan Oduya is canonically already dead (he died in the 1979 fire and haunts the app as the HO-9 dispatcher), so the character sheet's 'alive' status is an error. Taking the instruction literally, by erasing his ghost, would gut the HO-9 midpoint reveal, Ruth's final package, and the ledger. Instead, I put his death on-page in episode 10. The mitten's pickup routes Dessa to the ninth floor, and there she witnesses, in full, an unnamed superintendent dying against the jammed stairwell door, with a key tag stamped '9' as the clue. Episode 8 seeds this with a microfiche mention of an unnamed super who died on nine. Episodes 11, 12, 14, and 18 add small ripples: Gideon flinches, Dessa connects the '9' to HO-9, the dispatcher leaves her question unanswered, and Ilse reacts to the description, which foreshadows her guilt without revealing it. His name stays withheld, so the ep-96 identity payoff, the ep-123 chained-door payoff, and all act turning points are unchanged. The stairwell-door mystery now surfaces visually earlier than its ep-38 thread opening. The remaining beats (5–7, 9, 13, 15–17, and 19–24) are unchanged.
